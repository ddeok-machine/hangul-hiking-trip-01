## 2. Text ✅

```
HALLASAN SUNRISE 산악회 — OCTOBER OUTING

06:00  Meet at the bus. Bring 막걸리. Bring more 막걸리.
10:30  Snack break by the 오리 pond.
       Do NOT feed the 오리 your 오이. They bite.
11:00  Head count. (Last year: 23 went up, 19 came down.)
11:30  Regroup at the STONE TOWER at the top of the trees.
       Don't knock it over. Mr. 동 stacked it himself.
12:00  Take the trail behind the tower up to 백록담.
13:00  Group photo. Everyone smile.
       If you hear something big in the trees, keep smiling.

(handwritten at the bottom, smudged)
3 of us are still at the stone tower. They're waiting for you.
```

The printed schedule and the handwritten line should look different (typed versus scrawled, smudged) in the note view.

## 5. Seeds and cameos

- **"Mr. 동 stacked it himself"**: a sideways cameo by **동철수** 🟡, who builds cairns as well as reporting his poop.
- **"23 went up, 19 came down"** and **the smudged last line**: the dark kicker. Who are the three at the tower?
- **"Something big in the trees"** foreshadows the bear.
- **"12:00 … up to 백록담"** names the next stop: 오리, water, and the white deer.
